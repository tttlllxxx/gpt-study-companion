# GPT 学习伴侣图标

使用 Codex 内置 image_gen 生成。原始 PNG 保留透明通道；build.sh 使用 sips 缩放并用 iconutil 打包为 macOS ICNS。

## 最终提示词

+Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production macOS app icon for GPT 学习伴侣, a personal Markdown reading and AI question-answering app.
Primary request: Create one beautiful, original app icon combining an open book and a conversation bubble into one unified, immediately readable symbol.
Scene/backdrop: A single softly rounded square app tile, with genuinely transparent canvas outside its silhouette. No presentation board or device mockup.
Subject: An ivory-white open-book form whose right page subtly becomes a speech bubble. One restrained four-point insight sparkle integrated near the book's upper fold. A bold compact mark with balanced negative space.
Style/medium: Premium macOS icon with sculpted soft depth, crisp generous curves, subtle ceramic or frosted-glass surface treatment, carefully restrained highlights and shadows. Contemporary, calm, inviting, suited to focused study.
Color palette: Take the violet accent used by the app's reading links as the design cue; a luminous violet-to-indigo tile with ivory foreground and restrained cool highlights.
Composition/framing: Square image, straight-on view, exactly one centered icon filling roughly 84 percent of the canvas, optically balanced. The emblem must remain clear at 32 pixels.
Constraints: No letters, words, numbers, watermarks, border labels, extra decorative objects or existing brand logos. Render an actual transparent background outside the rounded tile. The tile itself must be visually substantial, with a clean silhouette and no wide atmospheric shadow. Deliver the icon artwork alone.
